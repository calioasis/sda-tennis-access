Campaign update

August 18, 2026

Parks and Rec heard from SDA Tennis Access.

SDA Tennis Access spoke at the Encinitas Parks and Recreation meeting. This was an important step toward getting the city side of the conversation moving.

Updates

  • I spoke at the Encinitas Parks and Recreation meeting about opening the San Dieguito Academy public school tennis courts during appropriate non-school hours.
  • I handed out campaign cards so people could quickly find the website, see the agreement examples, and understand that this is a researched, practical proposal.
  • The message stays the same: school use comes first, the agreement examples already exist, and this is a solvable city-school partnership.

View the Instagram update

Next step

  • Encinitas City Council meets August 19, 2026. The goal is to keep building awareness and move the city toward working with SDUHSD on an agreement.

We have school district momentum. Now we need the city side at the table so we can work toward a real agreement.

We are providing solutions, not problems. The courts are already there. The agreement models already exist. Now we need enough community support to help the City of Encinitas and SDUHSD make this happen.
