Campaign update

August 19, 2026

SDA Tennis Access spoke at City Council.

SDA Tennis Access spoke at the Encinitas City Council meeting, following the Parks and Recreation Commission meeting the night before. The conversation is now where it needs to be: with the City and the school district looking at a practical path to public access.

The practical next step

The request is for the City to identify the appropriate staff member to evaluate whether a joint-use arrangement with SDUHSD could make the San Dieguito Academy courts available during appropriate non-school hours.

SDUHSD has indicated a willingness to discuss a joint-use agreement. A City staff review can evaluate access, operations, maintenance, liability, cost, and scheduling before any decision about a pilot or funding is made.

The courts are already there. The agreement models already exist. Now the City and SDUHSD can determine whether there is a workable way to expand recreation access while keeping school use first.
